# flaui-cli

## Commits

Every commit subject follows Conventional Commits: `type(scope): summary`, with a lowercase type, an imperative summary and no trailing period.
Types in use are `feat`, `fix`, `docs` and `chore`, with `chore(deps)` for dependency updates; `refactor`, `test`, `build` and `ci` apply where they fit.
A breaking change takes `!` after the type and a `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer.
